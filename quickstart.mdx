## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
Before you begin, ensure you have a valid business email address and basic agency details ready, including your agency name, primary contact information, and preferred currency settings.
</Callout>

## Create Your ParyatechOS Account

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign up for an account" icon="user-plus">
Visit the registration page and enter your business email. Verify your account using the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
</Step>
<Step title="Complete your profile" icon="user">
Provide your agency name, address, and tax details. Select your primary operating region to configure default currency and time zone.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Your Agency Workspace

After signing in, create your first workspace by selecting **New Workspace** from the dashboard. Name it after your agency, such as `Sharma Travels HQ`.

<CodeGroup tabs="cURL,JavaScript,Python">
```bash
curl -X POST https://api.example.com/workspaces \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name":"Sharma Travels HQ","region":"asia-south"}'
```
```javascript
const response = await fetch('https://api.example.com/workspaces', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY',
    'Content-Type': 'application/json'
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    name: 'Sharma Travels HQ',
    region: 'asia-south'
  })
});
```
```python
import requests

response = requests.post(
    'https://api.example.com/workspaces',
    headers={'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY'},
    json={'name': 'Sharma Travels HQ', 'region': 'asia-south'}
)
```
</CodeGroup>

## Add Your First Team Members

Invite colleagues directly from the workspace settings. Assign roles such as `Admin`, `Agent`, or `Viewer` based on their responsibilities.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Web Dashboard" icon="monitor">
Navigate to **Team** {'>'} **Invite Members**. Enter email addresses and select appropriate roles for each team member.
</Tab>
<Tab title="API" icon="code">
Use the team invitation endpoint to programmatically add users with specific permissions.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Import Basic Vendor and Client Data

Upload your existing spreadsheets or connect to supported CRMs. ParyatechOS accepts CSV files for vendors and clients.

<CodeGroup tabs="CSV Vendors,CSV Clients">
```csv
name,category,contact_email,location
Grand Hyatt,Hotel,reservations@hyatt.com,Bali
Taj Palace,Hotel,bookings@taj.com,Delhi
```
```csv
name,email,phone,trip_type
Sharma Family,sharma@email.com,+919876543210,Luxury
Gupta Corp,contact@gupta.com,+911234567890,Corporate
```
</CodeGroup>
