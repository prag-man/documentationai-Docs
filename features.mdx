## Overview

ParyatechOS unifies every part of your travel agency operations into a single workspace. You manage active pipelines, maintain a structured vendor database, track customer queries, generate proposals, and automate follow-ups without switching between spreadsheets and messaging apps.

## Pipeline Management

You track every deal through customizable stages. The system automatically updates status based on actions like sending proposals or receiving payments.

## Query Tracking

Every customer inquiry lands in a structured queue. You capture requirements such as traveler count, budget range, travel dates, and special requests.

<Callout kind="info">
  Tag queries with labels like `Luxury`, `Corporate`, or `Family` to route them to the right team member automatically.
</Callout>

## Creating Proposals

Generate professional proposals directly from pipeline data. The system pulls vendor rates and itinerary details into formatted documents.

````bash
curl -X POST https://api.example.com/proposals \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "pipeline_id": "pl_9k2m4p",
    "client_email": "sharma.family@email.com",
    "template": "luxury-bali"
  }'
````

## Automated Workflows

You set rules that trigger actions without manual intervention. Examples include sending follow-up emails after three days of inactivity or creating tasks when a proposal reaches the negotiation stage.

<Expandable title="Example Workflow Rule" default-open="false">
  When a proposal status changes to `Sent`, automatically schedule a follow-up task for 48 hours later and notify the assigned agent.
</Expandable>

These features work together so you spend less time on coordination and more time closing trips.
