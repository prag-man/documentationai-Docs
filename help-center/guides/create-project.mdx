## Create Your First Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Dashboard">
    ````
    Log in to https://app.vimaksh.com and access your dashboard.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Project'">
    ````
    Find the 'New Project' button in the sidebar and click it.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Details">
    ````
    Enter project name, description, and select travel service options.
    Submit to create.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
