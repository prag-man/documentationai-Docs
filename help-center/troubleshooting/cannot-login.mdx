---
title: Cannot Log In
description: Troubleshooting login issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Unable to sign in at `app.vimaksh.com`
- "Invalid credentials" message

## Possible Causes

- Incorrect email or password
- Account not activated

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    ````
    Click "Forgot Password" at login and follow the instructions emailed to you.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check activation">
    ````
    Make sure you confirmed your registration email. Check spam folder.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
